WEEKLY FUEL MARKET BRIEFING: Week ending 31 May 2025
=================================================================

MARKET OVERVIEW
------------------------------
The WA unleaded petrol market averaged 173.4 cpl this week, with a 16.7 cpl spread between the cheapest brand (Metro, 160.1 cpl) and the most expensive (Shell, 176.8 cpl). Prices were broadly stable week on week.

7-ELEVEN POSITION
------------------------------
7-Eleven averaged 169.4 cpl this week, ranking 2 out of 7 active brands from cheapest to most expensive. This is 4.0 cpl below the market average. Week on week, 7-Eleven's price moved down by 0.3 cpl.

ANOMALY FLAGS
------------------------------
No brands are currently pricing more than 2 cpl outside their 8-week average. Market behaviour is within normal range.
